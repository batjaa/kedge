Kedge ingests RFC/spec documents from wherever they live (GitHub, Confluence, raw URLs), renders them beautifully, and layers first-class commenting, approvals, and AI review tooling on top.

**This site is the dogfood spike**: Kedge's own project documents, rendered by the Fumadocs shell that will become Kedge's reading surface. The comment rail on the right is a static fixture of the M2 design.
